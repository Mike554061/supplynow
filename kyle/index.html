Two ways to get your order

Hand us the shopping. Choose how it gets to you.

Every online order starts on RestaurantDepot.com, and our team selects and prepares it inside the depot. At checkout you choose pickup or delivery. Here is what changes from shopping in-store.

How most members shop today

In-store

You do every step yourself, and it all comes out of your day.

  • Drive to the depot and park
  • Walk the aisles and hunt for items
  • Load the cart and the flatbed
  • Wait in line to check out
  • Load your vehicle, drive back, unload

Online ordering hands these steps to us →

Fulfillment type 1

Pickup

Your order is selected, checked and staged before you arrive. You come to the depot and leave with it.

  1. Order onlineShop RestaurantDepot.com and choose pickup, with the date and time that suit you.
  2. We pull itOur selectors pick every item on your order, cold and frozen kept in temperature.
  3. It's waitingYour order is staged and ready when you arrive. Grab it and get back to work.
Best for

Owners already passing the depot, smaller or last-minute orders, and anyone who wants their order in hand today.

  • Skip the aisles, carts and lines
  • No more hunting for out-of-place items
  • In and out, with no time lost in the aisles
Fulfillment type 2

Delivery

Your order is selected, loaded and brought to your door on SupplyNow trucks. Nobody on your team leaves the building.

  1. Order onlineShop RestaurantDepot.com and choose delivery at checkout.
  2. We pull and load itSelected, checked and loaded onto temperature-controlled trucks.
  3. At your doorDelivered to your business with proof of delivery, cold chain intact.
Best for

Busy kitchens, bigger and recurring orders, and teams that can't spare a person or a vehicle for the depot run.

  • Your staff stays in the kitchen
  • No vehicle, fuel or loading on your end
  • Same days, same window, week after week

Who is SupplyNow, and how are you connected to Restaurant Depot?

SupplyNow is the team that selects, prepares and fulfills online orders at nine Restaurant Depot locations. You order and keep your account with Restaurant Depot; we pick your order and get it to you.

Do I need a Restaurant Depot membership?

Yes. Online orders are placed under your Restaurant Depot membership, which is free for qualifying businesses. See "Getting started" above for what to bring.

How do I place an order?

On RestaurantDepot.com or the app. Choose your depot, build your list, and pick pickup or delivery at checkout. Kyle can walk you through your first one.

Pickup or delivery: which is right for me?

Pickup suits owners who pass the depot anyway or need something today. Delivery suits busy kitchens, larger and recurring orders, and teams without a spare person or vehicle. You choose each time you check out.

Do you keep frozen and refrigerated items cold?

Yes. Cold and frozen product is handled to HACCP food-safety standards for both pickup and delivery.

Which locations do you serve?

Cleveland, Akron and Columbus, Ohio; Indianapolis, Indiana; Pittsburgh and Wilkes-Barre, Pennsylvania; and Troy, Ypsilanti and Dearborn, Michigan.

Something's wrong with my order. Who do I contact?

Kyle. Call or text 330-523-8748 or email kyle@supplynow.org with your order details and he'll make it right with the team.
